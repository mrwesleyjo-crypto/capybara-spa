Privacy Policy — Capybara Spa: Collect & Chill

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Capybara Spa: Collect & Chill (“the app”) on Android or iOS. The app is developed by Wesley de Vries (“I”, “me”), an independent developer based in the Netherlands.

1. Your game progress

The app has no user accounts and no own server. Your coins, upgrades, collected capybaras and settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device. I do not receive or store this data.

Cloud save (Android, Google Play Games). If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app also stores a copy of your game save in your Google Play Games account (Google's “Saved Games” service), so you can continue on a new or reset phone. This copy contains only game data (such as coins, upgrades, collected skins, settings and play time) and is linked to your Play Games profile by Google. I cannot see it. Google Play Games itself may process your Play Games profile and gameplay activity under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com. If you are not signed in, your progress stays on your device only, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app only shows optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward, such as a temporary 2× boost or doubled offline earnings. There are no forced ads. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location);
  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking);
  • interactions with ads (for example ad views and taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance).

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time via Shop → Ad privacy choices in the app. On iOS you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (RevenueCat, Google Play, Apple)

The app offers optional in-app purchases: one-time items (Double Income Forever, the Royal VIP Pass, the Starter Pack, complete skin sets and the seals Taylor and Travis), Pearls (an in-game currency for blind boxes) and optional “treats” that are only a thank-you to the developer. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; I never see your payment details. To check and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history (such as product, time of purchase and store receipt) together with an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email address from the app.

More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Seal adoption (Taylor and Travis). All my proceeds from the seal purchases (what I receive after Google's fees and VAT) go to Zeehondencentrum Pieterburen, the seal rescue centre in the Netherlands. This is a purchase of an in-game item, not a donation made by you; no personal data is shared with the rescue centre.

4. Reminder notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules at most two local notifications on your device when you leave the game: one when your spa has finished earning offline coins, and one when your daily gift is ready. They are never sent at night. These notifications are created and stored only on your device; no data is sent to me or to any other party. You can turn them off at any time in Stats → Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

5. Sharing

If you use the share button or add the capybara sticker pack to WhatsApp, the app hands the image or stickers to the app you choose on your phone; I do not receive anything.

7. Legal basis (GDPR)

Personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time via Ad privacy choices. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and purchase processing are based on legitimate interest and on performing the purchase you requested. The cloud save is part of the game service you use when you are signed in to Google Play Games.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. Because I do not store personal data myself, requests about ad, cloud-save or purchase data are best directed to Google and RevenueCat via the links above; you can also contact me and I will help. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
