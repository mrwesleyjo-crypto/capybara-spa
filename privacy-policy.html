Privacy Policy — Capybara Spa: Collect & Chill

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Capybara Spa: Collect & Chill (“the app”) on Android or iOS. The app is developed by Wesley de Vries (“I”, “me”), an independent developer based in the Netherlands.

1. Your game progress stays on your device

The app has no user accounts and no own server. Your coins, upgrades, collected capybaras and settings are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. I do not receive or store this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes your progress.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch an ad to get a temporary 2× boost. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location);
  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking);
  • interactions with ads (for example ad views and taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance).

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time via Shop → Ad privacy choices in the app. On iOS you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (RevenueCat, Google Play, Apple)

The app offers optional in-app purchases (the Royal Capybara skin and an ad-free 2× boost). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; I never see your payment details. To check and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history (such as product, time of purchase and store receipt) together with an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email address from the app.

More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Reminder notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules at most two local notifications on your device when you leave the game: one when your spa has finished earning offline coins, and one when your daily gift is ready. They are never sent at night. These notifications are created and stored only on your device; no data is sent to me or to any other party. You can turn them off at any time in Stats → Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

Personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time via Ad privacy choices. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and purchase processing are based on legitimate interest and on performing the purchase you requested.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. Because I do not store personal data myself, requests about ad or purchase data are best directed to Google and RevenueCat via the links above; you can also contact me and I will help. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
